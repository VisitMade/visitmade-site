# VisitMade marketing site — project status

Last reviewed: 1 October 2026

## Purpose

Explain VisitMade's destination websites, CRM, membership management and reporting proposition and convert appropriate interest into an enquiry/demo journey.

## Current state

A static marketing site exists with VisitMade branding, product explanation, illustrative product UI examples and links to the public website/CRM demo.

No analytics or enquiry form is currently enabled, and the agreed commercial contact/booking destination has not been recorded.

## What works

- Responsive marketing site.
- VisitMade brand treatment.
- Product tabs/content.
- FAQ and accessibility-conscious interactions.
- Static build/deployment workflow.
- Links to the existing demo surfaces.

## Live / deployment status

A public static site is available through the repository's current hosting path.

## Current milestone

**Turn the site into a credible route from interest to enquiry/demo once the commercial contact path is agreed.**

## Key decisions

- Marketing claims must be grounded in actual `VisitMade/platform` capability.
- Illustrative UI/content must not be presented as customer proof.
- No unverified pricing, compliance certification or testimonials.
- Avoid unnecessary runtime complexity for the marketing site.

## Current blockers / dependencies

- Agree the primary CTA.
- Agree sales/contact/demo destination.
- Decide whether analytics are needed now and under what consent/privacy setup.
- Decide whether enquiry is email, form, calendar/booking or another approved route.

## Next actions

1. Agree the primary conversion CTA.
2. Agree its destination/owner.
3. Add the enquiry/demo path.
4. Add analytics only if there is an agreed measurement/consent approach.
5. Recheck product claims against current platform status.
